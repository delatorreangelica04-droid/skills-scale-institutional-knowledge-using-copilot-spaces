# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Program Sponsor/Executive Champion

### Role Summary
Program Sponsors provide strategic oversight and executive support for project initiatives. They remove organizational blockers, approve key decisions, and champion change management across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and vision alignment
- Remove organizational blockers and resource constraints
- Approve budget and major project decisions
- Champion change management and organizational adoption
- Escalate issues beyond Project Manager authority
- Ensure project alignment with business strategy

### Goals
- Enable successful project delivery through executive support
- Remove barriers to team progress
- Ensure organizational commitment and buy-in
- Drive strategic value realization

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Project Manager**: Provides strategic oversight and decision authority; reviews major milestones and risks
- **Consulted by Project Manager**: On escalations requiring executive intervention; for budget or resource decisions
- **Supports Developers**: Provides organizational context and removes blocking dependencies
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Ensures product priorities align with business strategy

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Major milestone and gate reviews
- Escalation resolution and decision logs
- Organizational communication and change management announcements

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and enforce quality standards across deliverables. They conduct reviews, manage defect tracking, and ensure that all outputs meet established quality criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria frameworks
- Conduct quality reviews and audits on deliverables
- Manage defect tracking and resolution workflows
- Ensure test coverage and testing strategy alignment
- Report quality metrics and trends to Project Manager
- Collaborate on deployment readiness assessments

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet quality standards
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Provide clear visibility into quality status
- Support continuous improvement of quality processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Developers**: Reviews code quality; collaborates on testing strategies; provides feedback on implementation
- **Reports to Project Manager**: Quality status, defect metrics, and risks; recommends release readiness
- **Collaborates with Technical Architect**: Ensures solution design supports quality requirements
- **Partners with Release Manager** (when introduced): Validates deployment readiness; confirms quality gates

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews and code review participation
- Defect tracking and status reports
- Quality metrics dashboards
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Compliance & Security Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance & Security Officers ensure that projects adhere to regulatory requirements, security policies, and organizational governance standards. They manage security reviews and document compliance obligations throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure regulatory and compliance requirement adherence
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Document compliance requirements and controls
- Manage security risk identification and mitigation
- Maintain audit trails and compliance documentation
- Advise on data protection and privacy requirements

### Goals
- Ensure projects meet all regulatory requirements
- Minimize compliance and security risks
- Maintain organizational audit readiness
- Embed security and compliance into delivery processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Consulted by Project Manager**: During project planning and risk assessment phases
- **Works with Risk Manager**: Identifies and tracks compliance-related risks; coordinates mitigation strategies
- **Reviews with Developers**: On security implementation; data handling practices; code security
- **Partners with Quality Assurance Lead**: Integrates security and compliance testing
- **Supports Release Manager** (when introduced): Validates security controls before deployment

### Typical Communication
- Security and compliance review meetings
- Risk registers and compliance documentation
- Security requirement specifications
- Audit and compliance reports

---

## Stakeholder Communication Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Communication Leads manage engagement across all project stakeholders. They coordinate communications, gather feedback, manage expectations, and ensure consistent, transparent information flow throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute stakeholder communication plans
- Coordinate and deliver project communications and updates
- Gather and synthesize stakeholder feedback
- Manage stakeholder expectations and concerns
- Facilitate communication between technical and business teams
- Support retrospectives and lessons-learned sessions

### Goals
- Maintain clear, consistent communication with all stakeholders
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and buy-in
- Quickly identify and address stakeholder concerns
- Build organizational trust in project delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager**: On stakeholder status, feedback, and communication effectiveness
- **Collaborates with Program Sponsor**: On executive communications and change messaging
- **Works with Product Managers**: Shares product vision and priorities with stakeholders
- **Coordinates with all teams**: Gathers updates and perspectives for stakeholder communications
- **Supports retrospectives**: Facilitates feedback collection from team members and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder communication plans and schedules
- Project status communications and newsletters
- Feedback collection and stakeholder surveys
- Retrospective facilitation and lessons-learned documentation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical vision and strategic guidance on solution design for complex initiatives. They review architectural decisions, identify technical risks and dependencies, and ensure solutions align with technical strategy and scalability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical vision and solution architecture
- Review solution designs for alignment with technical strategy
- Identify and document technical risks and dependencies
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs
- Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability of solutions
- Mentor Developers on architectural best practices

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound, scalable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Guide teams toward long-term technical strategy
- Identify and mitigate technical risks early

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Developers**: During planning and execution; reviews technical designs; provides architectural guidance
- **Advises Project Manager**: On technical feasibility, complexity estimates, and technical risks
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead**: On architecture for testability and quality
- **Works with Compliance & Security Officer**: On security architecture and compliance-ready design
- **Consults with Product Managers**: On technical viability of product requirements

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical design documentation
- Risk identification and mitigation plans
- Technical guidance and mentoring sessions
